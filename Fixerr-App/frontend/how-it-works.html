How Fixerr works

Simple, transparent, and safe. No app required. No payment upfront. Our team personally confirms every booking.

1

Select your service category

Browse 12 service categories on fixerr.com. Prices are automatically shown in your local currency — ₹ INR for Indian cities, $ USD for US cities. Select your city at the top to update all prices.

2

Choose the specific service

Each category has 4–8 sub-services with individual pricing. Select exactly what you need — for example, under Plumbing you'd choose "AC Service" or "Faucet Repair". Don't see your service? Use the Custom Request option to describe anything.

3

Submit your request

Fill in your preferred date/time, full address, and your phone number. This takes under 2 minutes. You can also add any promo codes here for a discount.

📍 Full street address + city is required so we can assign a professional near you.
4

Our team calls you within 2 hours

This is what makes Fixerr different. Our team personally calls you to confirm the booking, give you the professional's details, and answer any questions. No automation — real humans.

5

Professional arrives and does the job

Our verified, ID-checked professional arrives at your address and completes the service. All professionals are rated by previous customers, so you know you're getting someone trusted.

✓

Pay only after the job is done

You pay the professional directly once the job is complete and you're satisfied. No upfront payment, no hidden charges. The price is set and confirmed by the professional before work begins.
